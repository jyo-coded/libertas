# Forum: the Libertas investigator console

**Scope:** the whole investigator SPA (message log, voucher, incidents, actors, graph, money, explorer, evidence, sources, Lex).
**Mode:** Operate.

**Audience and job:**
- Tamil Nadu Cyber Crime Wing officers triage what needs action now, read facts apart from leads, act (acknowledge, evidence pack), and follow actors, wallets and incidents.
- Judges watch on a projector and up close on the laptop.

**Constraints:**
- Text only; never render captured markup.
- Personal data appears masked.
- Leads stay visibly distinct from facts.
- Offline: self-hosted fonts and icons.
- Times in IST.
- SIMULATION must be unmistakable.

**User-pinned direction (beats the roll):** a fusion of Message Precedence (urgency, time, acknowledgement) and Passbook & Clearing (verdicts, evidence, money).

**Memorable moment:** an alert is logged UNCLEARED in pencil and, about a second later, the deep lane's ink stamp lands in place: CLEARED or RETURNED, with a reason.

**Unresolved:** Tamil labels (undecided).

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The console is a police signals log whose entries clear like cheques. Precedence (FLASH / IMMEDIATE / PRIORITY / ROUTINE) and the date-time group decide what needs action now; the deep lane's verdict lands as a clearing stamp. It refuses the category default of KPI cards stacked over a severity table.

**OWN-WORLD:**
- Shell: an olive-khaki signal rail on a grey-green message-pad ground, with white vouchers.
- Ink: near-black for facts; pencil graphite for leads and uncleared entries.
- Precedence tags: FLASH red, IMMEDIATE amber, PRIORITY blue, ROUTINE slate.
- Clearing stamps: violet CLEARED, maroon RETURNED.
- Type: Public Sans, with Red Hat Mono for DTGs, codes, hashes and figures.
- Simulation runs under an EXERCISE banner.

**STORY:** The officer sees unacknowledged FLASH and IMMEDIATE messages first and watches each one clear or return. They open its voucher, read the ink facts apart from the pencilled leads, then acknowledge it, build the evidence pack or follow the lead into the graph or the money passbook.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:**
- Top: the EXERCISE strip, then a header with unacknowledged precedence counts, collection state, time-to-detect, the IST clock and the kill switch.
- Left: the olive navigation rail.
- Message log (about 58%): ranked by precedence, then DTG, with a clearing stamp column; source health under it.
- Voucher (about 42%): clearing, facts, leads, Next actions.
